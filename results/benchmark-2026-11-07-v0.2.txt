Interpretation:
- The dynamic persistent runtime is slower than the sequential reference on these tiny integer DAGs.
- Approximate sequential/runtime median ratios: 0.109x, 0.219x, and 0.217x. Values below 1 mean the causal runtime is slower for this workload.
- Persistent worker startup is excluded, but per-event queueing, cloning, scheduling, channel, and bookkeeping overhead remains.
- This is not evidence that causal-DAG execution is generally slower; it is evidence that the current implementation does not pay off for cheap integer operations on these graphs.
- Future workloads must include skewed task costs, fan-out, wide frontiers, and realistic computation. Compare against mature DAG/dataflow baselines.
