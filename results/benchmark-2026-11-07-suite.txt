Interpretation:
- The dynamic runtime is slower than the sequential evaluator in all 15 measured cases.
- The seq/runtime ratio is below 1 in all cases; this ratio is sequential median divided by runtime median.
- The wider graphs reduce but do not eliminate the relative overhead.
- The graph families isolate chain depth, independent ready-frontier width, balanced fan-in, fan-out, and a skewed critical chain.
- The current engine is a correctness/semantics and research platform, not a demonstrated high-performance scheduler.
- The next optimization study should compare compact array-backed graph storage, lower-overhead dispatch, batching, sequential cutoffs, and realistic operation costs. Keep all scheduler variants semantically equivalent and benchmark worker-pool startup separately.
