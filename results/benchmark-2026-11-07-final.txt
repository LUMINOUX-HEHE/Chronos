Interpretation:
- The dynamic runtime is slower than the sequential reference in all 15 cases.
- seq/runtime is sequential median divided by causal-runtime median; all values below 1 mean sequential wins on these workloads.
- The highest observed ratio is 0.239x for a balanced tree at width 1024; the runtime remains about 4.2 times slower at the median.
- A compact fixed-size worker input snapshot reduced per-task data structure overhead but did not remove channel/scheduler/bookkeeping costs.
- This is a negative performance result for small pure integer operations, not a claim that causal scheduling is generally slower.
- Further work should compare a sequential cutoff, adaptive scheduling, and compact array-indexed graph storage, and add operations with meaningful computation cost.
